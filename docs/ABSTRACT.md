# Submission Abstract — AMP Challenge 2027 (draft)

## Method summary

We submit a two-generator autoregressive ensemble with panel-aware ranking.
Peptides are generated by a custom transformer decoder (6 layers × 384
hidden, block-wise attention residuals, 10.7M parameters) trained on the
provided antibacterial corpus with the confirmed recipe (100 epochs,
patience 10). Two checkpoints are deployed: an unconditioned model selected
from a 10-seed sweep for distributional coverage, and a charge-conditioned
variant whose per-sequence conditioning bins are drawn from the reference
charge histogram at sampling time — correcting a systematically narrow
generated charge distribution (pool σ 2.4 → 3.15 vs reference 3.28). The
final 50,000-sequence library is a deterministic 3:1 interleave of the two
pools, which outperforms both parents simultaneously in embedding-space
distributional metrics.

Ranking of the top-100 is multi-objective: a composite of property-conformity
density, an embedding-space precision proxy, and — principally — predicted
activity breadth from a genus-level multi-label classifier head over the
20-strain competition panel (10 genera; MDR-containing genera weighted
explicitly). The ranker is an ESM-2 (t12, 35M) backbone with a trained head,
trained on 13,485 labeled sequences derived from 69,461 strain-level DBAASP
MIC measurements (unit-normalized, banded at 4/16/32 µM). The deployed
scorer's macro AUROC is 0.81 (validation split; deployment equals training
by construction via frozen-backbone head-only checkpoints); an
identity-clustered split of the fine-tuned reference model scores 0.808,
bounding homolog-leakage at −0.055. Under this instrument the submitted
top-100 achieves predicted activity against effectively all panel genera
(breadth 0.997, MDR-genera 1.0, mean calibrated probability 0.79), and a
hemolysis-risk audit of the top-100 (separately trained HC50-band head, 0.85
AUROC) shows no concentrated safety risk.

Library quality, scored with the seqme protocol at an ESM-2 650M embedder:
FBD 0.221, MMD 0.357, Recall 0.899, Precision 0.863, Conformity 0.507,
Diversity 0.848, Novelty/Uniqueness 1.0. We note honestly that the training
corpus coincides with the evaluation reference set (permitted; emission
rules are enforced by exact-overlap and ≤80%-identity novelty screens), so
distributional metrics are best read as fidelity to the known AMP
distribution rather than out-of-sample generalization.

Reproducibility: `uv run generate` (defaults, seed 42) reproduces the library
and top-100 byte-for-byte (verified end-to-end, including from a fresh
clone). All training data, licenses, and retrieval provenance are disclosed
in docs/DATA_DISCLOSURE.md; all experiments — including null results — are
recorded in PHASE1_RESULTS.md.
